# Imaginode

> Imaginode (https://imaginode.ai) is a browser-based AI creation studio: users generate images, videos and voice-overs on a node canvas by wiring boxes together: text, image, video, camera, reference, style and audio nodes. 74 AI models (Flux, Seedream, Recraft, Kling, Seedance, Veo, GPT-5, Claude, Gemini, Kimi, OpenAI TTS…) are available on a single account with shared credits. Nothing to install; generation runs server-side, and it works fully on mobile browsers. The interface is available in 14 languages (French by default, then English, Spanish, German, Portuguese, Indonesian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Hindi, Russian, Turkish and Hebrew). A public REST API and a hosted MCP server expose the same pipeline, described in https://imaginode.ai/apis.json.

## How pricing works

1 credit ≈ $0.012 ($0.01 in the euro area). The exact cost of every generation is displayed on the Generate button BEFORE clicking. Failed generations (provider crash or timeout) are refunded automatically within seconds. An image costs 1–7 credits; a 5-second 720p video costs roughly 26–60 credits depending on the model; voice-over costs 5 credits per 1,000 characters.

### Subscriptions
- Starter: $17/month (€13 in the euro area), excl. tax, for 900 credits
- Creator: $55/month (€42 in the euro area), excl. tax, for 3,100 credits
- Studio: $188/month (€145 in the euro area), excl. tax, for 10,500 credits

### One-time top-ups
- 300 credits: $7 (€5 in the euro area), one-time, no subscription
- 1,000 credits: $20 (€15 in the euro area), one-time, no subscription
- 2,000 credits: $38 (€29 in the euro area), one-time, no subscription
- 3,000 credits: $55 (€42 in the euro area), one-time, no subscription
- 5,000 credits: $90 (€69 in the euro area), one-time, no subscription
- 10,000 credits: $175 (€135 in the euro area), one-time, no subscription

Credit lifetime: subscription credits cover the monthly period paid for and reset to the plan allowance at renewal (unused ones expire, as at Higgsfield or Runway). Credits bought as one-time top-ups, trial credits and referral bonuses never expire. Generations spend subscription credits first.

Cancel anytime from the dashboard.

## Key features

- Node canvas: each creation step is a box wired to others; every node keeps its generation history; auto-save; ready-made templates.
- AI assistant (1 credit/message) that builds complete workflows added to the canvas in one click.
- Character consistency via Reference nodes: @mention a character in any prompt and its photos are attached (Seedream for images, Seedance for video, up to 9+ reference images).
- Camera node: framing, lens (mm), aperture, angle and movement written into the prompt like a cinematographer would.
- Magic wand (1 credit): rewrites any prompt into rich, optimized English while preserving @mentions.
- Voice-over node: OpenAI TTS HD, 6 voices (male/female), adjustable speed, MP3 download.
- Image editing: one click creates a Flux Kontext node pre-wired to any image.
